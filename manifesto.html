● Manifesto

The rest of India isn’t a side quest.

No Yakult? That’s a market failure.

India’s consumer market extends far beyond its metros, but most brands haven’t figured out how to reach it. Not because people aren’t buying, but because entering a new city takes more than putting products on shelves. It takes local relationships, market understanding, demand generation, and consistent execution. Building all of that from scratch, city after city, is expensive and difficult.

We think Tier-2 and Tier-3 India is vastly underrated. But believing in its potential isn’t enough. Expanding across these markets without the right local infrastructure is wishful thinking.

That’s why we’re building Tractionn: a network of local commercial nodes that helps brands enter, understand, and grow in markets beyond the metros. By nodes, we mean anyone or anything that can help move products off shelves and into people’s hands: local SMBs, retailers, creators, event organisers, and other community-driven businesses. We bring these capabilities together into a coordinated network, giving brands access to local reach and execution without having to build everything from scratch.

We believe a brand’s growth shouldn’t be limited by the geography it can afford to operate in. And a consumer’s access to that brand shouldn’t be determined by their pin code.

We’re building the infrastructure to make growth beyond metros possible.

Vaibhav Rawat Founder, Tractionn
